## 1. Executive Summary

**CITYLINE CONSULTANCY** is a United Arab Emirates (UAE)-focused consultancy providing premium services across three foundational business verticals:
1. **Visa Services** (Freelance Visa assistance and Visit Visa processing).
2. **Business Setup** (UAE Company Formation and Setup assistance).
3. **Recruitment / Manpower** (Connecting vetted blue-collar and commercial talent with UAE employers).

The primary initiative is the delivery of a state-of-the-art, high-converting, accessible, and secure digital platform. The platform consists of two principal components:
- A **Public Web Platform** engineered with a sleek modern design, dynamic lighting modes (Light/Dark), glassmorphic elements, cinematic hero visuals, and responsive conversion flows.
- A **Protected Administrative Platform** facilitating multi-tenant lead management, secure applicant document handling, dynamic job and category publishing, client testimonial management, and privacy-conscious visitor analytics.

### 3.1. Visa Services
- **2-Year Freelance Visa Assistance**: Guidance and administrative coordination for self-sponsored residency in the UAE.
- **30-Day Visit Visa**: Short-term tourist/entry permit processing and documentation support.
- **60-Day Visit Visa**: Extended entry permit processing for individuals and families.
- *Strict Rule*: No pricing displayed. Exact document prerequisites depend on ongoing regulatory updates from UAE General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) and Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP).

4. **Zero Affiliation Claims**: Do not state or imply that Cityline Consultancy is a government entity or possesses official government partnerships unless certified documentation is supplied.
5. **Private Intermediary Status**: All public copy must accurately present Cityline Consultancy as a private consultancy and intermediary advisory firm.
